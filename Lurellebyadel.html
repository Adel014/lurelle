Meet the Girl Behind Lurelle 🎀

hii, I'm Adel! 💕 Lurelle is my little crochet world where I get to turn yarn, random ideas, (and way too much time😭) into cute handmade pieces. I started crocheting because I wanted to create things that were truly my own, and honestly, I ended up loving it way more than I expected.

Everything here is handmade by me, from the first stitch to the finishing touches, with lots of love. I hope each piece feels personal and special to whoever gets it. Welcome to Lurelle — I'm so happy you're here. ♡
